control-boundary min 3, 0 fails

The edge of an input, checkbox or radio is the only thing that shows where the control is, so WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3 to 1 against what is beside it. border/strong is the token for that job. border/primary and border/secondary divide content and are exempt (see 'exempt').

focus-ring min 3, 0 fails

The focus ring is drawn ring/offset away from its control, so what sits beside it is the surface, not the control's fill (WCAG 2.4.13, 1.4.11). It must reach 3 to 1 against every surface it can be drawn on.
