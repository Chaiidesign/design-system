# explore/

Everything in this folder is clay: made to learn from, disposable, never imported by brick and never released to real users.

- `visual-directions/`: three prototypes of the same checkout screen, for choosing the system's visual direction (work item 2.1). After the choice, the two not chosen are deleted. On 15 November the CEO chose Instrument for version 0.2; the Ledger and Signal pages were removed once 0.2 passed its checks, and their rationale notes are kept.
- `register.md`: the register of EXPLORE items. It is created with the first item.
