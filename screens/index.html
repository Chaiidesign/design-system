Souvenir design system 0.2

Every page follows the theme of your device. To see one in the dark theme, add ?theme=dark to the end of its address, or ?theme=light for the light theme. To see the teal accent in place of the blue, add ?accent=teal. To ask for both, join them with an ampersand: ?theme=dark&accent=teal.
