# A named check is not a check

- **What happened:** The lead's first drafts of charter items 1.1 to 1.4 were reviewed by three fresh reviewers. None of the five files passed. The most serious pattern: LOCK rows named a check ("writing check", "review checklist") that could not detect the breach, and rules were written in the present tense as if the checks already ran. Evidence: DECISIONS.md D-006.
- **Cause:** The lead wrote each rule and then asked "what kind of check would this be?" instead of "what fact would the check read, and where is that fact declared?". The lead also did not test its drafts against the gate's written pass criteria before asking for review.
- **Change made:** (1) Every LOCK row now says what declared fact its check reads, and checks are tracked as named, built or proven by catching a planted breach. (2) Before any of the lead's work goes to review, the lead runs the phase's pass criteria as a checklist and fixes what fails. (3) Every charter and docs file opens with a line saying what does not exist yet.
- **Scope and reason:** Applies to every rule the system claims to enforce, and to all of the lead's own work. A rule nobody can detect a breach of is a wish.
- **Delete when:** Every LOCK row's check is proven, and the lead's first-pass review rate is above one half across ten items.
